Everything it does

Check a deal

  • /check Price history and a verdict for any shop link: Real discount, Smaller than claimed, or Not a discount.
  • Right-click → Apps → Check this deal Check any message that contains a shop link - works in every server, even without the bot installed.
  • /proof An evidence file with every price we saw, with date and time - for complaints and returns.

Get told

  • /track A message the moment the price drops or your size is back in stock - usually within minutes. Set a target price if you like.

  • /watch A whole shop: every drop, restock and new product, optionally filtered by a keyword.
  • /list · /untrack What you follow, what it cost when you started, and what the whole list costs today.

Find and compare

How it works

  1. Paste a link - or right-click any message and choose Check this deal. Works in any server, including ones where the bot is not installed.
  2. We look up the history - 408 shops and about 1,235,000 offers, checked around the clock since September 2026, growing every week.
  3. You get a verdict - Real discount, Smaller than claimed, Not a discount or Too early to tell, with the chart to back it up. Share the picture.

What makes it trustworthy

Checked daily against the shops themselves: on 2026-09-21, 29 of 29 sampled prices matched exactly (1 more had changed since our last look).

  • It only says what it saw. Not enough history means “too early to tell” - never a guess dressed up as a verdict.
  • Every verdict carries its dates. You can check our work.
  • It reads only what shops publish for machines, honours robots.txt and never works around a block. Shop owners can opt out with one line.
  • It has nothing to sell you. No affiliate links, no ads, no sponsored deals. The verdict cannot be bought.
  • It judges prices, not shops. We report what we observed. What it means is for you to decide.
