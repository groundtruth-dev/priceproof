For shop owners

If RealPriceBot showed up in your logs: hello. We record public prices so shoppers can see whether a sale is a sale. Shops with honest pricing come out of this looking good - a list price that matches the 30-day low gets a green REAL stamp on every check.

How we read your shop

  • Only the endpoints you publish for machines: the product feed (/products.json, WooCommerce Store API), your product sitemap and the schema.org product data on product pages.
  • robots.txt first, always. A 403 or 429 pauses us for 24 hours. No proxies, no disguised client.
  • One full read every 30 minutes at most; single pages a few per minute with pauses, usually far less.
  • Your shop is read once, no matter how many people track it.

Want to be listed accurately?

Tell us about a product feed we should use instead, a launch you would like watched, or a price we got wrong: priceproof.contact@gmail.com. We answer within one working day.

Prefer not to be included?

Add this to your robots.txt, or write to priceproof.contact@gmail.com from an address at your shop's domain:

User-agent: RealPriceBot
Disallow: /

Applied within one working day, permanently. Past observations of your shop are no longer shown.
